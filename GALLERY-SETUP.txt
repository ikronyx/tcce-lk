TCCE website – Gallery installation notes
=========================================

The ZIP provided for editing contained the advert images but NOT the files in
images/gallery/ca, images/gallery/cel, or images/gallery/hah.

Before publishing, copy the complete folders from your laptop into:

    images/gallery/ca/
    images/gallery/cel/
    images/gallery/hah/

Keep ALL filenames unchanged, including spaces in the 'cel' folder.
The website is already configured for the exact 37 + 13 + 7 images you listed.

Until the photos are copied, album previews show neutral placeholders and the
modal explains why the selected photo is missing. This is intentional, not a
broken external image or spinner. The eight adverts have been converted from
WebP to JPG and their image/link URLs updated; the originals are retained.

Gallery controls: click album, use previous/next arrows, arrow keys, touch
swipes on mobile, or small thumbnail strip, and close with Escape or X.
All interactions are implemented locally with js/tcce-gallery.js, no CDN,
third-party dependency, or Internet connection needed.

Check by opening index.html or serving the entire folder on a normal web host.
Do NOT upload just index.html: upload css, js, images, and media directories too.
