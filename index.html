About TCCE

A stronger foundation for a global future.

The College Of Cambridge English Examinations (TCCE) is an accredited Cambridge English Examination Preparation Centre and a registered British Council Sri Lanka registration centre serving learners across the surrounding regions.

More than exam preparation.

TCCE focuses on building practical communication ability, learner confidence and examination readiness through engaging and structured English education.

Cambridge Focused Preparation aligned with recognised Cambridge pathways.
Age 5+ Programmes designed for young learners and school students.
Three Locations Mirigama, Giriulla and Warakapola.
Placement Testing Find the right starting level before enrolling.

Recognition that matters.

TCCE has been recognised at a British Council national awards ceremony with a High Achiever Certificate in appreciation of achieving the highest regional candidate registrations for Cambridge English examinations.

Speak With TCCE
Why choose TCCE?

TCCE combines Cambridge-focused preparation with learner-friendly teaching approaches designed to make English more enjoyable, practical and confidence-building.

What skills are developed?

Learners work across Reading, Writing, Listening and Speaking, with activities structured around their Cambridge level and learning stage.

Where does TCCE operate?

TCCE serves learners through its main locations in Mirigama, Giriulla and Warakapola.

Can new students take a placement test?

Yes. Prospective students can complete a placement assessment to help identify an appropriate benchmark level before joining a programme.
